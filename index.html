Step 1 — Diagnostics & Cash-Flow Restructuring

Optimized household spending structure, cutting 23% of living expenses (down to 50 million VND/month) while preserving 100% of education costs for the children. Result: cash flow shifted from deficit to a net surplus of +6.7 million VND/month.

Step 2 — Debt Repayment Strategy

Recommended retaining the Bao Loc land asset (long-term appreciation potential) and committing to a fixed repayment of 8.3 million VND/month over 6.1 years, instead of a loss-making fire sale.

Step 3 — Investment Policy Statement (IPS)

CFA-compliant risk tolerance assessment yielded a Moderate profile (24/40 points). SMART goals defined, 5-year liquidity constraint set, tax and legal constraints for banking-sector employees fully accounted for.

Step 4 — Portfolio Optimization

Allocated 100% to the Sharpe-maximizing equity portfolio: ACB 63% · FPT 15% · REE 12% · IDC 10% — delivering an expected return of 12.72% per annum with a superior Return-per-Unit-of-Risk.

Step 5 — Results Achieved

Completed 100% of the roadmap: car purchase in the 500–800 million VND segment (after 3 years), home purchase in District 2 / District 9 (after 10 years) — on schedule.
